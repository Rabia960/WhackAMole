Privacy Policy

Tuli’s Whack-a-Thon · Last updated: November 2026

The short version: the game has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. Your player name and your best scores are stored on your device. If you choose to play on the shared leaderboard, your name and best scores are also saved to an online database so other players can see them. Nothing else is sent.

Who I am

Tuli’s Whack-a-Thon is made by Rabia Saya (“I”). You can contact me at rabiamustafa.saya@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your device

To remember your game, it saves these things in your browser or app storage on your own device:

  • the player name you type (please use a nickname, not your full name)
  • your chosen character, difficulty level and sound setting
  • your best scores
  • a random player ID the game creates, which is not linked to your identity or your Google or phone account
  • a short log of the last 20 technical errors, which helps fix bugs

The game also keeps a copy of its own files on your device so that it loads fast and works offline. You can remove all of this at any time by clearing the site data for the game or by uninstalling the app.

Shared leaderboard

The game shows a leaderboard of the best scores from all players. To make this work, the following is saved to an online database hosted by Google (Firebase Cloud Firestore) each time you set a new best score on a level:

  • the player name you typed
  • the level and your best score on that level
  • the random player ID described above, and the time of your score

Anyone who opens the game can see these names and scores on the leaderboard. Please don’t type your real full name, your phone number, your address, or anything else personal as your player name. Use a nickname.

If you want your scores removed from the shared leaderboard, email me (see Contact) with the name you played under. I will delete those entries. Players can’t delete their own entries from the game itself.

“Report a problem” button

This button only does something when you press it. It prepares a short message with the game version, your character and level, your screen size, your browser or device type and the recent error log. It opens your phone’s share menu so that you choose where to send it. It is never sent to me automatically.

Hosting and third parties

The game’s files are delivered by Vercel, a web hosting company. Like most hosting providers, it may keep standard technical server logs (for example an IP address and the time of a request) when your device downloads the game files.

The shared leaderboard stores its data with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Google processes that data under its own privacy policy. I don’t use advertising or analytics services in the game.

Children

The game is meant to be enjoyed by players of all ages. Please ask a parent or guardian before a child plays, and make sure they use a nickname rather than their real name. A child’s name and scores appear on the public leaderboard if they play online. If you are a parent and want a child’s entries removed, contact me and I will delete them.

Your choices

  • Change or delete the name and scores saved on your device by clearing the site data for the game, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Remove your entries from the shared leaderboard by emailing me (see Contact).
  • Turn off the “Report a problem” sharing by simply not pressing the button.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and the date at the top.
