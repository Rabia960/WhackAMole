Privacy Policy

Tuli’s Whack-a-Thon · Last updated: November 2026

The short version: the game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send your name, scores or anything else to a server. Everything it remembers stays on your own device.

Who I am

Tuli’s Whack-a-Thon is made by Rabia Saya (“I”). You can contact me at rabiamustafa.saya@gmail.com.

What the game stores on your device

To remember your game, it saves these things in your browser or app storage on your own device:

  • the player name you type (please use a nickname, not your full name)
  • your chosen character, difficulty level and sound setting
  • your best scores
  • a random player ID the game creates, which is not linked to your identity
  • a short log of the last 20 technical errors, which helps fix bugs

The game also keeps a copy of its own files on your device so that it loads fast and works offline. None of this is sent to me. You can remove all of it at any time by clearing the site data for the game or by uninstalling the app.

Leaderboard

At the moment the leaderboard only shows scores saved on your own device. If I ever add a shared online leaderboard, it would store a display name, scores and the random player ID in a cloud database. I would update this policy before that starts.

“Report a problem” button

This button only does something when you press it. It prepares a short message with the game version, your character and level, your screen size, your browser or device type and the recent error log. It opens your phone’s share menu so that you choose where to send it. It is never sent to me automatically.

Hosting and third parties

The game’s files are delivered by Vercel, a web hosting company. Like most hosting providers, it may keep standard technical server logs (for example an IP address and the time of a request) when your device downloads the game files. I don’t use advertising, analytics or any other third-party services in the game.

Children

Because the game does not collect personal information, it does not collect any from children either. Everything it saves stays on the device it is played on. Children should use a nickname if they type a name.

Your choices

  • Change or delete your saved name and scores by clearing the site data for the game, or by uninstalling the app.
  • Turn off the “Report a problem” sharing by simply not pressing the button.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your privacy, I will update this page and the date at the top.
